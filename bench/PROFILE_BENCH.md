## Notes

- `endpoint_events` is exactly 2x the number of completed range scans; `segments` never exceeds the number of distinct end points, regardless of interval width.
- `sparse-int64` covers the entire int64 key domain (-2^63..2^63-1) with three intervals and only six end-point events: cost grows with end points/records, never with key-domain width.
- Heat counters are aggregated in bounded memory and spilled to SQLite above the in-memory cap; no interval is expanded into per-key records.
- `verify_ok` means `verify-profile` independently recomputed every signed field from the raw history.
